## How the Pipeline Works (Quick Overview)

```
Pick Channel + Topic → Launch Pipeline → AI Generates Brief → AI Writes Script
→ Human Approval → Voiceover (F5-TTS) → Visuals (ComfyUI/Flux) → Upscale
→ Stitch (FFmpeg) → QC Check → Publish
```

1. **You** select a channel and type a topic
2. **AI** generates a research brief (titles, thumbnail concepts, duration)
3. **AI** writes a full narrator-led script (HOOK, INTRO, BODY, CTA)
4. **You** review and approve at the Approval stage
5. **System** synthesizes voiceover, generates keyframes, assembles video
6. **System** runs QC and publishes to the target platform

## Channel Details

### 1. MLN — Macro Lens

**Objective:** Become the go-to YouTube source for actionable macro-economics and crypto investment insights. Translate complex sovereign debt, central bank policy, and crypto market dynamics into clear, visual explainers.

**Niche:** Macroeconomics, global finance, cryptocurrency, wealth preservation, central bank policy, gold vs bitcoin, yield compression.

**Target Audience:**
- Demographics: Males 28-55, household income $75K+, US/UK/AU
- Psychographics: Self-directed investors concerned about inflation and currency debasement
- Viewing context: Desktop and mobile, evening hours
- CPM potential: $18-22 (finance/brokerage display ads at premium CPM)

**Content Strategy:**
- Narrative style: Authoritative, data-driven, urgency without hype
- Visual style: Dark navy/gold palette, financial charts, cinematic documentary
- Episode structure: Hook (personal stakes) → Data → Framework → CTA
- Recurring segments: "The Macro Picture," "Positioning Framework," "Weekly Macro Brief"

**Production Plan:**
- Weekly: 2 long-form videos (8-12 min each)
- Monthly: 8 videos total + 4 YouTube Shorts from key segments
- Quarterly theme refresh based on market conditions

**Business Prospects:**
- Primary: AdSense at $18-22 CPM (finance niche premium)
- Secondary: Gold IRA company sponsorships, crypto exchange partnerships
- Tertiary: Wealth preservation checklist PDF (lead magnet), trading platform referral codes
- Revenue estimate: $2,000-5,000/mo at 50K views/video

**Rationale:** Finance content has the highest CPMs on YouTube. Central bank policy + crypto convergence is a timely, evergreen topic with massive search volume ("inflation hedge 2026" up 340% YoY). Few quality explainers combine macro + crypto + gold.

## How to Launch a Pipeline

1. Go to **Pipeline Dashboard** (`/pipeline`)
2. Click **"How It Works"** to read this guide
3. Select a **Target Channel** from the dropdown
4. Type a **Topic** (be specific: "AI Agent Infrastructure 2026" > "AI")
5. Click **"Launch Pipeline"**
6. Watch the pipeline progress through stages in the table
7. When status shows **pending_approval**, review and click **Approve**
8. The pipeline continues automatically through voiceover, visuals, stitch, QC, publish
