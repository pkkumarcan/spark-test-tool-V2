## Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: YouTube AdSense (Primary)

**Requirements:**
- YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Tier 2: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours
- Enable monetization in YouTube Studio
- Link AdSense account

**Revenue model:**
- YouTube places ads on your videos
- You earn revenue based on CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions)
- Revenue varies by niche, season, and audience location

### Stream 3: Sponsorships (Growth Stage)

**When to pursue:** 50,000+ subscribers per channel

**Typical rates:**
- 10K-50K subs: $100-500 per integration
- 50K-100K subs: $500-2,000 per integration
- 100K+ subs: $2,000-10,000+ per integration

**Sponsorship types:**
1. **Integrated mention** (30-60 seconds in video): Most common
2. **Dedicated video** (entire video about product): Highest pay
3. **Channel sponsor** (logo in all videos): Recurring revenue

**How to get sponsors:**
1. Build media kit with channel stats
2. Reach out to relevant brands
3. Join influencer platforms (Grapevine, Channel Pages)
4. Let sponsors come to you (at scale)

## Monetization Roadmap

### Phase 1 (Month 1-3): Build Foundation
- Focus on content quality and consistency
- No monetization yet (pre-YPP)
- Cost: $45/mo (tools + electricity)

### Phase 2 (Month 3-6): First Revenue
- Apply for YPP Tier 2 (1,000 subs + 4,000 hours)
- Enable AdSense on qualifying channels
- Add affiliate links to descriptions
- Expected: $150-600/mo

### Phase 3 (Month 6-12): Scale Revenue
- All Phase 1+2 channels monetized
- Pursue first sponsorships (at 50K+ subs)
- Launch first digital product
- Expected: $700-2,500/mo

### Phase 4 (Month 12+): Full Operation
- All 10 channels monetized
- Multiple revenue streams active
- Sponsorship deals in place
- Digital products selling
- Expected: $5,000-18,000/mo
