## Comment Management

### Response Strategy

| Comment Type | Response Time | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Genuine question | <24 hours | Answer thoughtfully |
| Positive feedback | <48 hours | Thank them, ask follow-up |
| Constructive criticism | <24 hours | Acknowledge, explain reasoning |
| Trolling | Ignore | Don't engage, mark as spam if needed |
| Spam | Immediate | Delete + block user |

### Response Templates

**Answering a question:**
```
Great question! [Answer]. I actually cover this in more detail in
my video on [related topic] — I'll link it in the cards.
Thanks for watching! 🙏
```

**Thanking for positive feedback:**
```
Thanks so much! glad this resonated with you.
What topic would you like me to cover next?
```

**Handling constructive criticism:**
```
Fair point — I appreciate you sharing that perspective.
[Explain reasoning or acknowledge the gap].
I'll keep this in mind for future videos.
```

### Pin Strategy

Pin a comment on every video:
- **Option A:** Key takeaway from the video
- **Option B:** Question to drive engagement ("What's your experience with this?")
- **Option C:** Link to related video or resource

## Engagement Tactics

### In-Video Engagement

**Opening hook (first 30 seconds):**
- Ask a question: "Have you ever wondered why...?"
- Make a bold claim: "This one habit changed everything..."
- Create curiosity: "By the end of this video, you'll know..."

**Mid-video engagement:**
- "Comment below if you've experienced this..."
- "Pause and try this right now..."
- "If you're finding this helpful, hit like..."

**End screen CTA:**
- "The next video builds directly on what we just covered"
- "Subscribe so you don't miss it"
- "Drop a comment with your biggest takeaway"

### Comment Prompting

End every video with a specific question:
- "What's your experience with [topic]?"
- "Which of these strategies will you try first?"
- "Did I miss anything? Let me know in the comments"
- "What topic should I cover next?"

### Reply Strategy

- Reply to every comment in first 24 hours (boosts algorithm)
- Heart good comments (shows engagement)
- Pin best comment (encourages quality)
- Pin your own comment with key takeaway or question

## Audience Growth Tactics

### Cross-Promotion

| Method | How | When |
|--------|-----|------|
| End screens | Link to next video | Every video |
| Cards | Link to related video | Mid-video |
| Playlist | Group related videos | Always |
| Community posts | Tease upcoming content | Before publish |
| Shorts | Clip best moments from long-form | 2-3x/week |

### Shorts Strategy

Create 60-second Shorts from long-form videos:
1. Take 1-minute "best moment" from each video
2. Export vertically (9:16)
3. Add captions (burned in)
4. Post as Short

**Benefits:**
- Shorts get algorithm boost
- 10M view threshold for Tier 2 YPP
- Viral potential is massive
- Drives traffic to long-form

### Collaboration

When to collaborate:
- At 10K+ subscribers (you have value to offer)
- With channels in similar niche but different angle
- With channels at similar subscriber count

How to collaborate:
- Guest appearance in each other's videos
- Shoutout exchanges
- Joint live streams
- Playlist features

## Channel-Specific Community Tactics

- **Community posts:** Study playlists, focus tips
